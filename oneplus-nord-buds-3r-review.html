OnePlus Nord Buds 3r

KAVORYN review: The OnePlus Nord Buds 3r are budget-focused true wireless earbuds with 12.4mm titanized drivers, long battery life, 3D Spatial Audio, AI call noise cancellation and low-latency gaming features.

★★★★☆ 4/5

Quick overview

The Nord Buds 3r are designed for everyday listening, calls and casual gaming. OnePlus lists up to 12 hours of playback from the earbuds and up to 54 hours with the charging case under its stated test conditions.

What we like 👍

  • Very strong advertised battery life for the price segment.
  • Large 12.4mm drivers aimed at a fuller sound.
  • Useful low-latency mode for gaming.
  • Dual-device connectivity is convenient for switching between devices.
  • IP55 rating adds useful protection for everyday use.

Things to consider 👎

  • These are budget earbuds, so expectations around premium ANC should be realistic.
  • Some advanced features can work best with compatible OnePlus phones or the relevant app.
  • Battery figures are manufacturer claims; real-world results vary.

Who should buy it?

The Nord Buds 3r make sense for someone looking for affordable TWS earbuds with long battery life, gaming-friendly latency and useful smart features.

If your priority is powerful active noise cancellation or premium audiophile features, consider a higher-end model.

KAVORYN verdict

The OnePlus Nord Buds 3r offer a strong feature set for a budget pair of earbuds. The combination of 12.4mm drivers, up to 54 hours of claimed total playback, Bluetooth 5.4, dual connection and 47ms low latency makes them an interesting option for everyday users and casual gamers.
